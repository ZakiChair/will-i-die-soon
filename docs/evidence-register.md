# Evidence register

Register audit date: **2026-08-03**, updated **2026-09-29** for the probable-conditions module (five new rules, twenty new sources, published screening instruments) and **2026-10-02** for completable estimates (ranges over missing answers, habit gains at an equal profile, per-instrument orientations, measurement help; five new sources). This document describes the exact checked-in private-research prototype. It is an inventory of transparent rule outputs and source consumption, not a claim that the product or any rule is clinically validated.

## Policy and release boundary

The running results route selects `prototypePolicy`: audience `private-research`, qualitative rules enabled, prompt-review and urgent signals enabled, and **validated probabilities enabled** since 2026-09-29. The flag `allowValidatedProbabilities` has two readers: the separate module `app/lib/pathology-risk.ts`, which re-implements published screening instruments and shows their published percentage only when the flag is true, and `policyAllows` in `app/lib/risk-engine.ts`, which would gate rules of evidence tier `validated-estimate` (no such rule exists today). `publicWellnessPolicy` keeps the flag `false`; under that policy the module still returns points and category but withholds every percentage. The public-wellness and regulated policies in source are separate policy foundations and are not active release modes. A public wellness release needs a new content, privacy, security, regulatory, and access review. Any regulated module additionally needs a named jurisdiction, frozen validated model version, exact eligibility, endpoint and horizon, test vectors, rights, quality/risk management, and the relevant conformity work.

All active rule outputs are deterministic checks over declared questionnaire IDs. There is no hidden model, remote inference, learned weighting, mortality predictor, life-expectancy estimate, or date-of-death estimate. No rule emits a numeric disease probability. The active rule inventory contains **zero `validated-estimate` rules**; a publication's presence does not activate a model. The only numeric risk outputs are the published bands and formulas listed under [Published screening instruments](#published-screening-instruments-pathology-module); they never pass through `RiskLeaf.copy`, so `assertEvidenceContract` is unchanged.

An evidence tier classifies what a specific output/rule is permitted to say. It does **not** label a publication as intrinsically “authoritative,” “guideline,” or “limited.” The same publication could support outputs with different allowed claims. Here, `authoritative-safety` permits a regulator/public-health safety warning without a probability; `guideline-action` permits a measurement, screening, support, or clinician-discussion action; and `evidence-limited-association` permits qualitative context only.

## Published screening instruments (pathology module)

`app/lib/pathology-risk.ts` (`PATHOLOGY_RULESET_VERSION = "pathology-scores-v2"`) computes the instruments below from structured answers, the profile and confirmed lab values, for adults only. Since 2026-10-02 adult Express results include them too, from the nine Express answers and any follow-up answers. Each result carries its `status` (`complete`, `incomplete` with named `missingInputs`, or `not-applicable` with a reason among `age-out-of-range`, `diagnosed-condition`, `established-cvd`, `sex-not-supported`, `region-not-calibrated`), its `inputs` with derived values flagged, and its `sourceIds`. Percentages are the instruments' own published bands or formulas and are emitted only when `policy.allowValidatedProbabilities` is true. "Unsure"/"unknown" answers count as missing rather than negative. Nothing here is a diagnosis, and none of the publications validates this product.

The dementia block also shows the `dementia_family_history` answer as context (`reported`, `not-reported` or `unanswered`). Family history is not one of the Commission's modifiable factors, so it never enters the factor count and carries no source of its own.

Three outputs added in `pathology-scores-v2` (2026-10-02) reuse the instruments above without new coefficients:

- **Range** (`incomplete` results only): when at most two declarative answers are missing, the instrument is re-run with every possible value of those answers (yes/no and single-choice options, and fixed probes on each side of the waist, neck, plant-food, weekly-activity and drinks thresholds). The range is shown only if every combination gives a complete result. Sex, height, weight, blood pressure and lab values are never enumerated. Percentages in a range follow the same policy flag as complete results. When both ends share a category, that category is settled and its orientation is shown.
- **Habit gain** (`complete` results only): the same instrument with the same age, sex, measurements and history, where only the declared unfavourable habits are replaced (FINDRISC daily activity and daily vegetables or fruit, CAIDE 150 weekly minutes, SCORE2 current smoking). Weight and waist are never changed. The output is a comparison of scores at an equal profile, not a causal effect or a promise.
- **Orientation**: one sourced next step per instrument and category (13 identifiers in `app/lib/pathology-orientation.ts`, with their source IDs). Negative AUDIT-C, PHQ-2 and GAD-2 screens and unsettled ranges carry none. Messages name "a doctor" generically; national resources are out of scope.

Published percentages are presented as "about N in 100" (per 1,000 below 1 %) with a decorative 10 × 10 grid. The [completable-estimates design](reviews/2026-10-02-estimations-completables-design.md) lists the probes, habit substitutions and orientation texts.

Test vectors in `app/lib/pathology-risk.test.ts` reproduce the ESC worked example for SCORE2 (man aged 50, smoker, systolic 140 mmHg, total cholesterol 6.3 mmol/L, HDL 1.4 mmol/L, low-risk region → 6.3%), FINDRISC 0, 15 and 26 points, STOP-Bang 2/3/4 with the male-sex escalation, the AUDIT-C, PHQ-2, GAD-2 and COPD-PS cut-offs, CAIDE 12 points → 16.4%, the withheld percentage under `publicWellnessPolicy`, and the empty synthesis for minors. Since 2026-10-02 they also cover the FINDRISC range with the waist missing (11–15 points, 4–33 %), the settled AUDIT-C range (positive, 4–12 points), the absence of a range beyond two missing answers or for blood pressure and labs, the FINDRISC gain (15 → 12 points, 33 → 17 %), the CAIDE gain (7.4 %), the SCORE2 smoking gain, and the complete orientation table.
