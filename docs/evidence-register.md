# Evidence register

Register audit date: **2026-08-03**, updated **2026-09-29** for the probable-conditions module, **2026-10-02** for completable estimates, **2026-10-04** for country-based cardiovascular estimates and **2026-10-07** for the scientific-review predictor update (Life's Essential 8 score, new mortality-predictor rules). This document describes the private-research prototype. It is an inventory of transparent rule outputs and source consumption, not a claim that the product or any rule is clinically validated.

## Policy and release boundary

The running results route selects `prototypePolicy`: audience `private-research`, qualitative rules enabled, prompt-review and urgent signals enabled, and **validated probabilities enabled** since 2026-09-29. The flag `allowValidatedProbabilities` has two readers: the separate module `app/lib/pathology-risk.ts`, which re-implements published screening instruments or reads the published WHO chart cell and shows the percentage only when the flag is true, and `policyAllows` in `app/lib/risk-engine.ts`, which would gate rules of evidence tier `validated-estimate` (no such rule exists today). `publicWellnessPolicy` keeps the flag `false`; under that policy the module still returns categories, and points when applicable, but withholds every percentage. The public-wellness and regulated policies in source are separate policy foundations and are not active release modes. A public wellness release needs a new content, privacy, security, regulatory, and access review. Any regulated module additionally needs a named jurisdiction, frozen validated model version, exact eligibility, endpoint and horizon, test vectors, rights, quality/risk management, and the relevant conformity work.

All active rule outputs are deterministic checks over declared questionnaire IDs. There is no hidden model, remote inference, learned weighting, mortality predictor, life-expectancy estimate, or date-of-death estimate. No rule emits a numeric disease probability. The active rule inventory contains **zero `validated-estimate` rules**; a publication's presence does not activate a model. The only numeric risk outputs are the published bands and formulas listed under [Published screening instruments](#published-screening-instruments-pathology-module); they never pass through `RiskLeaf.copy`, so `assertEvidenceContract` is unchanged.

An evidence tier classifies what a specific output/rule is permitted to say. It does **not** label a publication as intrinsically “authoritative,” “guideline,” or “limited.” The same publication could support outputs with different allowed claims. Here, `authoritative-safety` permits a regulator/public-health safety warning without a probability; `guideline-action` permits a measurement, screening, support, or clinician-discussion action; and `evidence-limited-association` permits qualitative context only.

## Published screening instruments (pathology module)

`app/lib/pathology-risk.ts` (`PATHOLOGY_RULESET_VERSION = "pathology-scores-v4"`) computes the instruments below from structured answers, the profile and confirmed lab values, for adults only. Since 2026-10-02 adult Express results include them too, from the Express answers and any follow-up answers. The Express index itself is `express-index-v2` (since 2026-10-07): VO₂ max is compared with FRIEND-registry age/sex percentiles (published for ages 20–79; 18–19 and 80+ read against the nearest band as an extrapolation), the 30-second chair stand is compared with Rikli-Jones age/sex normative ranges (references published for 60+; under 60 a guideline-based reading), and activity, sleep and nutrition are read against public guidelines. Its fixed references live in `app/lib/express-assessment.ts` and the Express block of `app/lib/export.ts`, outside this registry. Each result carries its `status` (`complete`, `incomplete` with named `missingInputs`, or `not-applicable` with a reason such as `age-out-of-range`, `established-cvd`, `outside-validated-range`, `sex-not-supported`, `region-not-calibrated`, `never-smoked`), its `inputs` with derived values flagged, and its `sourceIds`. Percentages use the published bands, formulas or printed WHO cells and are emitted only when `policy.allowValidatedProbabilities` is true. "Unsure"/"unknown" answers count as missing rather than negative. Nothing here is a diagnosis, and none of the publications validates this product.

Since 2026-10-07 FINDRISC carries a `findrisc-age-extrapolated` reading modifier when the computed age falls outside the validated 35–64 range; the points remain computable, and the modifier renders through the pathology copy. `pathology-scores-v4` (2026-10-07) adds the Lee index and PLCOm2012 and an "Estimated probabilities of major outcomes" block at the top of the Conditions section. The block lists, in a fixed order (all-cause death, cardiovascular disease, lung cancer, type 2 diabetes, dementia), every instrument that publishes a percentage, with its horizon, status (percentage, "estimate pending · N questions at most" from the follow-up planner, "blood test needed", or "not applicable to you") and the population it was validated in. Its introduction states that the list covers only outcomes for which a validated model exists and fits the data, that it is not a list of every cause of death, and that no number is an individual prediction. The follow-up planner now resolves compound (`any` / `all`) branch conditions: a hidden input is counted behind the unanswered eligible gate of its condition (for example the smoking details behind `current_tobacco_nicotine`, then behind `smoking_history_former` after a no), and the Lee index and PLCOm2012 declare their gated inputs (heart failure, COPD, the smoking details, the education level) as `conditionalInputs` so the question count stays an upper bound.

The dementia block also shows the `dementia_family_history` answer as context (`reported`, `not-reported` or `unanswered`). Family history is not one of the Commission's modifiable factors, so it never enters the factor count and carries no source of its own.

Three outputs added in `pathology-scores-v2` (2026-10-02) reuse the instruments above without new coefficients:

- **Range** (`incomplete` results only): when at most two declarative answers are missing, the instrument is re-run with every possible value of those answers (yes/no and single-choice options, and fixed probes on each side of the waist, neck, plant-food, weekly-activity and drinks thresholds). The range is shown only if every combination gives a complete result. Sex, height, weight, blood pressure and lab values are never enumerated. Percentages in a range follow the same policy flag as complete results. When both ends share a category, that category is settled and its orientation is shown.
- **Habit gain** (`complete` results only): the same instrument with the same age, sex, measurements and history, where only the declared unfavourable habits are replaced (FINDRISC daily activity and daily vegetables or fruit, CAIDE 150 weekly minutes, cardiovascular, Lee index and PLCOm2012 current smoking; for PLCOm2012 quitting today means former-smoker status with zero years since quitting). Weight and waist are never changed. The output is a comparison of scores at an equal profile, not a causal effect or a promise.
- **Orientation**: one sourced next step per instrument and category, with source IDs in `app/lib/pathology-orientation.ts`. Negative AUDIT-C, PHQ-2 and GAD-2 screens and unsettled ranges carry none. Messages name "a doctor" generically; the separate urgent-signal path handles national resources.

Published percentages are presented as "about N in 100" (per 1,000 for positive results below 1 %); a percentage that is 0 after rounding, such as a printed WHO 0 % cell, reads "under 1 %" and "fewer than 1 in 100". The 10 × 10 grid is decorative. The [completable-estimates design](reviews/2026-10-02-estimations-completables-design.md) lists the probes, habit substitutions and orientation texts; the [country-model design](reviews/2026-10-03-modeles-cardiovasculaires-par-pays-design.md) covers the additional source tables and boundaries.

## Supplemental Life's Essential 8 source

## Audit interpretation

The seven inactive records are `cdcStiTesting`, `fdaUnapprovedGlp1`, `fdaCompoundedRisks`, `cdcInjectionSafety`, `mhraCorticosteroids`, `fdaCompoundedSemaglutide` and `whoStepsPhysicalMeasurements` (added on 2026-10-02 as the basis of the waist-measurement help wording, which displays no source list). Inactive means “not consumed by a current rule or by the pathology module,” not invalid or unreviewed. The fourteen instrument, guideline and commission publications added on 2026-09-29 are consumed by the pathology module through `resolvePathologySources`, not by qualitative rules; the four NHS pages added on 2026-10-02 are consumed by its orientations. The twelve predictor sources added on 2026-10-07 are consumed directly by the ten new rules of the scientific-review update; the two instrument publications added the same day for `pathology-scores-v4` (Lee 2006, Tammemägi 2013) are consumed by the pathology module. The four DailyMed product labels are conditional-only. No source key used by a rule or by the pathology module is missing from the registry, and no duplicate URL exists within either registry. The single supplemental Life's Essential 8 URL does not overlap the evidence registry, producing the combined **120 active records / 120 unique URLs** total.
